# Car loan rules (MAS)
Source URLs:
https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/explainers/motor-vehicle-loans
https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2026/motor-vehicle-financing-restrictions-extended-to-individual-owned-private-hire-cars
Date notes were written: 2026-11-08 (summarised in own words from the MAS pages; the explainer page was updated 29 October 2026)

Who sets the rules: The Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) sets maximum limits on the amount and length of loans used to buy a new or used motor vehicle. This includes bank loans, licensed moneylender loans, hire purchase and conditional sale agreements.
Loan-to-value (LTV) limit when OMV is $20,000 or less: The maximum loan is 70% of the purchase price. The purchase price includes taxes and the COE price. So the buyer pays at least 30% upfront.
Loan-to-value (LTV) limit when OMV is more than $20,000: The maximum loan is 60% of the purchase price, including taxes and COE. So the buyer pays at least 40% upfront.
Maximum loan tenure: The maximum tenure for a motor vehicle loan is 7 years.
Which OMV counts: The limit tier is decided by the car's Open Market Value (OMV), not by the full price. A typical new family car has an OMV well above $20,000, so the 60% limit is the one that usually applies.
Worked example (illustration made for this project): For a car with a purchase price of $150,000 and OMV above $20,000, the most the bank can lend is 60%, which is $90,000. The buyer pays at least $60,000 in cash or CPF-free funds.
Rules history: The limits were tightened in February 2013 to 60% and 50% with a 5-year tenure, and eased on 27 May 2016 to the current 70% and 60% with a 7-year tenure.
What is not covered: The limits do not apply to motorcycles, commercial vehicles, vehicles for people with physical disabilities or their caregivers, and private hire cars not owned by an individual.
Private hire cars owned by individuals: From 30 October 2026, individuals buying private hire cars for their own use, including sole proprietors, are covered by the same limits. This applies when the sales agreement is signed on or after that date, or when the COE is obtained on or after that date if there is no sales agreement. Taxis, business-owned private hire cars and other commercial vehicles remain exempt.
Used cars: MAS adjusts the OMV of an older used car for its age when deciding which LTV tier applies. See the MAS page on the adjusted value of used motor vehicles for the method.
Refinancing: Refinancing has its own rules on tenure. Check the MAS page on refinancing rules for motor vehicle loans.
Zero-downpayment offers: Because of the loan limit, an offer that asks for no downpayment on a regulated car purchase is unusual. Check where the extra funding comes from and what the real interest cost is.
