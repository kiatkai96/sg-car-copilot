# VES, EEAI and electric car incentives
Source URLs:
https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2025/9/news-releases/extension_of_ves_and_eeai_to_support_vehicle_electrification.html

Date notes were written: 2026-11-08 (summarised in own words from LTA and NEA pages; per-band amounts for C1 and C2 come from a third-party summary and should be checked on OneMotoring)

What VES is: The Vehicular Emissions Scheme (VES) gives a rebate or charges a surcharge on new cars and taxis, based on their emissions. It has applied since 1 January 2018. It looks at five pollutants: carbon dioxide (CO2), hydrocarbons, carbon monoxide, nitrogen oxides and particulate matter.
How the VES band is decided: The worst performing pollutant decides the band. The band decides whether the buyer gets a rebate, pays nothing extra, or pays a surcharge. The amount is applied to the Additional Registration Fee (ARF).
VES extension: LTA and NEA announced on 8 September 2025 that the VES is extended from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2027, with revised bands, rebates and surcharges. The pollutant thresholds did not change. Only the band structure shifted.
VES bands from 2026: The old Band A2 became Band B (neutral, no rebate and no surcharge). The old Band B became C1, the old C1 became C2 and the old C2 became C3. Only electric cars get a rebate. Hybrids no longer get a VES rebate.
VES amounts for cars registered in 2026: Band A rebate of $22,500. Band B nil. Band C1 surcharge of $7,500. Band C2 surcharge of $22,500. Band C3 surcharge of $35,000.
VES amounts for cars registered in 2027: Band A rebate of $20,000. Band B nil. Band C1 surcharge of $15,000. Band C2 surcharge of $30,000. Band C3 surcharge of $45,000.
VES for taxis: Taxis have a 1.5 times multiplier on the car amounts.
VES label: Under the new rules, new cars that are subject to the fuel economy and emissions requirements must show the new VES label on the front windscreen when displayed for sale from 1 January 2026.

What EEAI is: The EV Early Adoption Incentive (EEAI) is a rebate for newly registered fully electric cars and taxis. It is 45% off the Additional Registration Fee (ARF).
EEAI cap and end date: For cars registered in 2026 the EEAI cap is $7,500, down from $15,000 before. The EEAI ends on 31 December 2026 and is not offered from 1 January 2027. Rebates are tied to the registration date.
Combined EV savings: For an electric car registered in 2026, the VES Band A rebate and the EEAI together can take up to $30,000 off the ARF ($22,500 plus $7,500). For an electric car registered in 2027, only the VES rebate remains, so up to $20,000.
ARF floor for electric cars: The ARF floor for electric cars and taxis is $0 and is maintained until 31 December 2027. For petrol cars an older LTA table showed a minimum ARF payment of $5,000 after rebates, so check OneMotoring for the current floor.
Why this matters for buyers: Electric cars registered in 2027 lose the EEAI and get a smaller VES rebate than in 2026, so the same car can cost more upfront in 2027. Hybrids lose VES rebates from 2026. Petrol cars with high emissions face higher surcharges that rise again in 2027.
EV road tax: From 1 January 2022 the road tax for fully electric cars in the 90kW to 230kW range was reduced so that electric and petrol cars of similar make and luxury level pay similar road tax. See the road tax explainer for the formula.
Other EV support: The Government aims for 60,000 EV charging points by 2030 and for all vehicles to run on cleaner energy by 2040. There is also an Electric Vehicle Common Charger Grant (ECCG) for charger deployment.
