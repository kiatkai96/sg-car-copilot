# ARF and OMV explainer
Source URL: https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/onemotoring/home/buying/upfront-vehicle-costs/tax-structure.html
Also see: https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/onemotoring/home/buying/upfront-vehicle-costs/additional-registration-fee--arf-.html
Date notes were written: 2026-11-08 (summarised in own words from the official OneMotoring page)

What OMV is: The Open Market Value (OMV) is the value of the vehicle assessed by Singapore Customs, based on the cost, insurance and freight of the vehicle. It is the starting point for several taxes.
What ARF is: The Additional Registration Fee (ARF) is a tax paid when a vehicle is registered. It is worked out as a percentage of the OMV, using tiers, so higher OMV cars pay a higher rate on the upper slices.
Registration Fee: There is also a fixed Registration Fee (RF) of $350 for a car.
Excise duty: Excise duty on a car is 20% of the OMV.
Other upfront costs: A car buyer also pays the COE Quota Premium (Category A, B or E) and road tax, on top of OMV-based taxes.
Used imported car surcharge: An imported used car must be no more than 3 years old when it is registered in Singapore, and a $10,000 used car surcharge applies.

Current ARF tiers for cars: These apply to cars with COEs obtained from the 2nd COE bidding exercise of February 2023 onward, and to cars that do not bid for COEs and are registered on or after 15 February 2023.
Current ARF tier 1: the first $20,000 of OMV is charged at 100%.
Current ARF tier 2: OMV from $20,001 to $40,000 is charged at 140%.
Current ARF tier 3: OMV from $40,001 to $60,000 is charged at 190%.
Current ARF tier 4: OMV from $60,001 to $80,000 is charged at 250%.
Current ARF tier 5: OMV of $80,001 and above is charged at 320%.

Older ARF tiers for cars (PARF-era): These apply to cars with COEs obtained before the 2nd COE bidding exercise of February 2023, and to COE-exempt cars registered before 15 February 2023.
Older ARF tier 1: the first $20,000 of OMV is charged at 100%.
Older ARF tier 2: OMV from $20,001 to $50,000 is charged at 140%.
Older ARF tier 3: OMV from $50,001 to $80,000 is charged at 180%.
Older ARF tier 4: OMV of $80,001 and above is charged at 220%.

ARF worked example (illustration made for this project, not an LTA example), current tiers: a car with an OMV of $30,000 pays 100% on the first $20,000 and 140% on the next $10,000, so ARF = $20,000 + $14,000 = $34,000.
ARF worked example, current tiers, higher OMV: a car with an OMV of $60,000 pays $20,000 + $28,000 + $38,000 = $86,000 ARF.
ARF worked example, older tiers: a car with an OMV of $60,000 pays $20,000 + $42,000 + $18,000 = $80,000 ARF.
Note: the ARF paid is what the PARF rebate is based on when a car is deregistered early. See the PARF rebate notes.
