# Motor insurance and No-Claim Discount
Source URLs:
https://gia.org.sg/motor-insurance/21-buying-a-motor/345-motor-insurance-consumer-guide.html (General Insurance Association of Singapore)
https://www.sc.com/sg/wealth-insights/why-car-insurance-matters
Date notes were written: 2026-11-08 (summarised in own words; premiums are not given because they vary by insurer, car and driver)

Insurance is compulsory: Under the Motor Vehicles (Third-Party Risks and Compensation) Act, a vehicle cannot be registered or driven on Singapore roads without valid insurance. At minimum, cover must include third-party death or bodily injury.
Three main policy types: Insurers offer three main types: Third Party, Third Party Fire and Theft, and Comprehensive.
Third Party: This covers death or injury to other people and damage to other people's property. It does not cover your own car. It is the legal minimum and the cheapest option.
Third Party, Fire and Theft: This covers the same as Third Party, and also covers your own car if it is damaged by fire or stolen.
Comprehensive: This covers all third-party claims, and also damage to your own car, including accident damage. It costs more than the other types. Cover for your own car is usually up to its market value at the time of claim.
Optional extras: Common extras include personal accident benefits, windscreen cover, towing, a replacement car and No-Claim Discount protection for private cars. Extras on comprehensive policies may cost additional premium.
Choosing for an older car: For an older, low-value car, comprehensive cover can cost more than the car is worth, so some owners choose Third Party only. This is a trade-off between premium and protection.
Workshop choice: Some plans let you repair at any workshop, others only at the insurer's authorised workshops. Authorised-workshop plans usually cost less.
What No-Claim Discount (NCD) is: NCD is a discount on your premium for each year you drive without making a claim on your policy. It builds up for each consecutive claim-free year.
NCD maximum: Under the General Insurance Association framework the maximum standard NCD is 50%. One insurer, DirectAsia, has offered a higher 60% tier for long-term claim-free drivers. Check what your insurer offers.
Losing NCD: Making a claim can reduce your NCD. Some policies offer NCD protection for drivers at the maximum NCD, which may allow a claim without losing the discount.
Common exclusions: Normal wear and tear, and damage caused by unauthorised drivers, are typical exclusions. Always read the policy wording.
Insurance and road tax: Valid insurance is needed to renew road tax and to complete an ownership transfer.
