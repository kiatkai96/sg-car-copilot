# Periodic vehicle inspection
Source URLs:
https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/onemotoring/home/owning/ongoing-car-costs/inspection.html
Date notes were written: 2026-11-08 (summarised in own words from the OneMotoring page; fines and fees come from secondary guides)

Why inspection exists: Vehicles must go for regular inspections to stay roadworthy. How often depends on the type and the age of the vehicle.
Private cars, by age: A private car under 3 years old does not need a periodic inspection. From 3 to 10 years old it is inspected every 2 years. Above 10 years old it is inspected every year.
Taxis and private hire cars: These follow different schedules. Since 1 January 2025 taxis under 3 years old are inspected annually instead of every 6 months, and chauffeured private hire cars above 10 years old are inspected every 6 months. Other types such as motorcycles, buses and goods vehicles have their own rows in the LTA table.
Inspection notice: LTA sends an inspection notice within 3 months before the inspection due date. You do not have to wait for the notice if the vehicle is already due.
Early inspection: If a vehicle is inspected more than 3 months before its due date, the result is not valid for road tax renewal. Contact LTA if there are exceptional reasons for an early inspection.
Road tax link: A valid inspection result is needed when renewing road tax for a vehicle that is due for inspection. Insurance must also be valid.
Repeat offenders: A vehicle that fails an inspection for certain offences more than once is put on an enhanced regime. A second-time offender must be inspected every 6 months for 2 years. A third-time or more frequent offender must be inspected every 3 months for 2 years.
Penalty for ignoring a notice: A secondary guide says that not sending a vehicle for inspection when due can lead to a fine of up to $1,000, or imprisonment of up to 3 months, or both. Check LTA for current penalties.
Illegal modifications: Improper car modifications can make a car fail inspection and can affect insurance cover.
Buying a used car: When buying a car older than 3 years, ask when its next inspection is due and whether it recently passed.
