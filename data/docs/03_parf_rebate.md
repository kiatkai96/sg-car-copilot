# PARF and COE rebate explainer
Source URL: https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/onemotoring/home/buying/rebates.html
Date notes were written: 2026-11-08 (summarised in own words from the official OneMotoring page)

What PARF is: The Preferential Additional Registration Fee (PARF) rebate gives back part of the ARF to a car owner who deregisters the car before it is 10 years old. It rewards owners for not keeping an old car.
Two rebates on deregistration: When a vehicle is deregistered, the owner may get a PARF rebate (for the ARF) and a COE rebate (for the unused COE).
PARF eligibility for private cars: The car must be new when registered in Singapore, or an imported used car that was no more than 3 years old when registered on or after 1 September 2007. It must be no more than 10 years old at deregistration and must never have been laid up.
PARF after COE renewal: Once a car is more than 10 years old the PARF rebate is nil. Renewing the COE to keep the car past 10 years means giving up the PARF rebate.

PARF rebate groups: The rebate schedule depends on when the car was registered.
Group 1: cars with COEs obtained before the 2nd bidding exercise of February 2023, and COE-exempt cars registered before 15 February 2023. There is no cap.
Group 2: cars with COEs obtained from the 2nd February 2023 exercise up to the 1st February 2026 exercise (or registered 15 February 2023 to 12 February 2026). The rebate is capped at $60,000.
Group 3: cars with COEs obtained from the 2nd February 2026 exercise onward (or registered on or after 13 February 2026). The rebate is capped at $30,000.

PARF rebate rates for Group 1 (percentage of ARF paid, by car age at deregistration): up to 5 years old 75%. Over 5 to 6 years 70%. Over 6 to 7 years 65%. Over 7 to 8 years 60%. Over 8 to 9 years 55%. Over 9 to 10 years 50%. Over 10 years nil.
PARF rebate rates for Group 2: the same percentages as Group 1 (75%, 70%, 65%, 60%, 55%, 50% by age band), but the rebate is whichever is lower of the percentage of ARF or $60,000.
PARF rebate rates for Group 3: up to 5 years old 30%. Over 5 to 6 years 25%. Over 6 to 7 years 20%. Over 7 to 8 years 15%. Over 8 to 9 years 10%. Over 9 to 10 years 5%. Over 10 years nil. The rebate is whichever is lower of the percentage of ARF or $30,000.
Net ARF: For vehicles that received tax incentives (for example the Green Vehicle rebate or VES/CEVS rebate), the PARF rebate is based on the net ARF paid.
PARF worked example (illustration made for this project): a Group 1 car that paid $34,000 ARF and is deregistered at 4 years old gets 75% = $25,500. The same ARF under Group 3 at 4 years old gives 30% = $10,200.

COE rebate: A COE rebate is given if the vehicle is deregistered before the COE expires. It is based on the Quota Premium paid (or the Prevailing Quota Premium for renewed COEs and taxis) and is pro-rated to the unused COE period.
COE rebate formula: COE rebate = Quota Premium of the 10-year COE x total unused months of the COE / 120.
COE rebate example from LTA: $16,897 x 29.1 months / 120 months = $4,097.
Exporting early: If a car is exported within 2 years of registration, the COE rebate is capped at 80% of the Quota Premium paid.
Category E COEs: the rebate uses the lower of the Category E Quota Premium paid or the Quota Premium of the vehicle's own category from the same bidding exercise.
Redeeming rebates: PARF and COE rebates are valid for 12 months from the deregistration date. The application to encash them must reach LTA within that time.
Checking your own rebate: LTA's OneMotoring has a digital service to enquire the PARF/COE rebate for a registered vehicle. Use it to confirm the exact rebate for a specific car rather than relying on a seller's figure.
