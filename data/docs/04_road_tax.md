# Road tax explainer
Source URLs:
https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/onemotoring/home/buying/upfront-vehicle-costs/tax-structure.html
https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/onemotoring/home/owning/ongoing-car-costs/road-tax.html
https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/dam/onemotoring/pdf/Circulars%20to%20ESAs/2021/VRL_20_2021.pdf (electric car formula)
Date notes were written: 2026-11-08 (summarised in own words from the official LTA pages)

What road tax is: Road tax is part of the vehicle ownership tax. Every registered vehicle in Singapore must have valid road tax. It can be renewed for 6 or 12 months through OneMotoring.
How petrol car road tax is decided: For petrol, petrol-CNG, CNG and diesel cars, road tax depends on the engine capacity (EC) in cc. Bigger engines pay more. The amounts below are for each 6 months. Annual road tax is double.
Petrol car road tax, EC up to 600cc: $200 x 0.782 per 6 months.
Petrol car road tax, EC over 600cc up to 1,000cc: [$200 + $0.125 x (EC - 600)] x 0.782 per 6 months.
Petrol car road tax, EC over 1,000cc up to 1,600cc: [$250 + $0.375 x (EC - 1,000)] x 0.782 per 6 months.
Petrol car road tax, EC over 1,600cc up to 3,000cc: [$475 + $0.75 x (EC - 1,600)] x 0.782 per 6 months.
Petrol car road tax, EC above 3,000cc: [$1,525 + $1 x (EC - 3,000)] x 0.782 per 6 months.
Road tax worked example (illustration made for this project): a 1,500cc petrol car pays [$250 + $0.375 x 500] x 0.782 = about $342 per 6 months, so about $684 a year. A 1,600cc petrol car pays [$250 + $0.375 x 600] x 0.782 = about $371 per 6 months, so about $743 a year.

Electric car road tax: Electric cars are taxed on the Power Rating (PR) of the motor in kW, not on engine size. For cars with more than one motor, the combined power of all motors is used. Formulas per 6 months (in force from 1 January 2022): PR up to 7.5kW is $200 x 0.782. PR over 7.5 up to 30kW is [$200 + $2 x (PR - 7.5)] x 0.782. PR over 30 up to 230kW is [$250 + $3.75 x (PR - 30)] x 0.782. PR above 230kW is [$1,525 + $10 x (PR - 230)] x 0.782.
Additional Flat Component (AFC): Electric cars also pay an Additional Flat Component as part of road tax at each renewal, because they do not pay fuel excise duty when they refuel. Check OneMotoring for the current amount.
Petrol-electric (hybrid) cars: Road tax is worked out twice, once from engine capacity and once from the electric motor's power rating. The higher of the two amounts is what must be paid.

Age surcharge: Cars more than 10 years old pay a surcharge on their road tax. More than 10 years old: 10% extra. More than 11 years: 20%. More than 12 years: 30%. More than 13 years: 40%. More than 14 years: 50%.
Late renewal: Renewing road tax late brings late fees and other consequences. LTA's example: a car under 1,000cc renewed 3.5 months after expiry pays a $230 late fee on top of the road tax.
Check the exact amount: OneMotoring has a digital service, Calculate Road Tax Renewal Rate, that gives the exact road tax from the vehicle number, or from engine capacity or power rating and vehicle age.
